How it works

Four steps from yesterday’s orders to tomorrow’s shelf.

01

Import

The delivery app’s order export and a simple stock file.

02

Diagnose

Short lines, deal-breakers, days of cover and busy hours.

03

Decide

Restock, delist, reorder sooner, reroute the picker.

04

Act

A daily message for the owner and a pick list for the team.

Every number traces back to the store’s own orders, so the team trusts the recommendation.

The insight that changes the game

Some items don’t just go missing. They cancel the whole order.

1 item

is enough to lose a full basket worth the store’s average order of ₪168.

Restock when an item caused 6 or more cancellations in 4 months and sells over 100 units a month: raise its shelf level.

Delist when it sells fewer than 100 units a month: mark it unavailable in the app before a customer orders it.
