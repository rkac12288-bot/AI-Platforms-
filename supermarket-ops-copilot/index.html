            Stock position

            On hand ÷ daily sales (Wolt + in-store) = days of cover, against the next delivery and shelf life
            stockout risk: cover < days to next delivery · markdown: fresh item with cover > 80% of shelf life · overstock: cover > max(21 days, 3 × reorder interval) · slow: ≤ 1 unit on Wolt in 14 days · stock value at 72% of Wolt price

            Refund and loss ledger

            Items that make customers cancel the whole order

            4-month history. An item is a deal-breaker when it was short and the customer then cancelled the full order.
            deal-breaker rate = cancelled orders where the item was short ÷ orders containing it · revenue at risk / month = cancellations per month × average basket (₪0)
            rule: ≥ 6 cancellations in 4 months and ≥ 100 units a month → restock (raise par) · fewer than 100 units a month → delist or mark unavailable on Wolt

            Your decision is saved in this browser and feeds the owner report.

            short rate = short lines ÷ lines ordered · new interval = max(1 day, current × (1 − min(0.8, 3 × short rate)))
