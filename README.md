# AI Platforms

A portfolio of AI-driven business platforms designed and built by **Renata**, AI Solutions Architect and Business Designer, with a background as a geologist and exploration manager.

Each platform starts from a concrete business problem — losses in a supermarket, compliance in a pharmacy, GenAI governance in a large holding, capital allocation in mining — and turns it into a working tool that people can open in a browser.

> All company names, people and figures in this repository are fictional or anonymised. No client data is included.

## Common approach

- **Single-file HTML apps** — no install, no backend; open and use
- **Vanilla JavaScript + Chart.js**, institutional design systems, light and dark themes
- **Built with Claude** as an AI pair-architect, from problem framing to working product
- **Regulation-aware** — LGPD, BACEN and sector rules designed in from the start
